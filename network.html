03 / CONNECTIVITY

Network field guide.

Understand common connectivity problems and work through basic checks in a safe order.

⌁WI-FI

Wi-Fi won't connect

Incorrect credentials, weak signal, router issues or wireless adapter settings may prevent connection.

Safe first checks

  1. Confirm Wi-Fi is enabled and airplane mode is off.
  2. Check the password and move closer to the router.
  3. Restart the router using its normal power procedure.
◌SPEED

Internet is very slow

Weak signal, network congestion, background downloads or service-provider issues can reduce speed.

Safe first checks

  1. Pause large downloads and streaming.
  2. Test another device on the same network.
  3. Compare results at different times and locations.
⌕DNS

Websites won't open

A DNS lookup issue, browser problem or unavailable website may prevent pages from loading.

Safe first checks

  1. Try a different website and browser.
  2. Check whether other devices can open the site.
  3. Restart the browser and device.
↯DROPS

Connection keeps disconnecting

Signal interference, router placement, adapter power settings or provider outages may interrupt service.

Safe first checks

  1. Move closer to the router.
  2. Check whether all devices disconnect.
  3. Check your internet provider's outage information.
Privacy note: This guide does not scan your network or collect Wi-Fi passwords. Never share your router administrator password with an unknown person.
